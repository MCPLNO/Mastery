# Mastery
From a single block of concrete to an AE2 network—a hardcore 1.21.1 NeoForge modpack where tech and magic alternate. No sieves, no ex nihilo. Early development.

你在一格混凝土上醒来。没有树，没有矿，没有筛子。

全部家当只有一台 Tier 1 赫菲斯托斯熔炉，以及两种颜色的混凝土：白色和浅灰色。这就是你的整个世界。

把混凝土变成气体，把气体变成合金，把合金变成机器，把机器变成 AE2 网络，再把网络变成能造出传送门的东西。

这不是一个“大杂烩”整合包。没有筛子，没有“一个方块”，没有凭空生成的资源——一切都要经过化学、仪式与自动化，一步一步转化而来。

科技与魔法交替推进。科技撞墙时，转向魔法；魔法开门后，再回到科技。如此循环，直到你能用橡木原木造出一扇传送门。

没错，橡木原木。Minecraft 里最普通的方块，就是你的终局材料。

你逃离了虚空孤岛，踏入一个全新维度。然后你问自己：

你真的逃出来了吗？还是只是进入了一座更大的岛？

You wake up on a single block of concrete. No trees. No ores. No sieves.

All you have is a Tier 1 Hephaestus Forge and two colors of concrete: white and light gray. That's it. That's your entire world.

Turn concrete into gas. Turn gas into alloys. Turn alloys into machines. Turn machines into an AE2 network. Turn that network into something that can build a portal.

This is not a kitchen-sink pack. There are no sieves. There is no "one block." Resources are not generated—they are converted, step by step, through chemistry, rituals, and automation.

Technology and magic alternate. You push tech until it hits a wall. Then you push magic until it opens a door. Then you push tech again. The loop continues until you can build a portal out of oak logs.

Yes. Oak logs. The most ordinary block in Minecraft is your endgame material.

You escape the void island. You step into a new dimension. And then you ask yourself:

Did you really escape? Or did you just enter a bigger island?
